Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to VoiceFlow. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our Chrome Extension handles data when you use our services.

2. Data Collection

VoiceFlow is designed with a "Privacy-First" approach. We do not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data to external servers.

  • No Personal Identification: We do not collect names, emails, or IP addresses.
  • No Tracking: We do not use cookies or tracking pixels to monitor your browsing history.
  • No Content Uploads: The text you select to be read aloud is processed locally on your device via the Web Speech API and is never sent to any server.

3. Local Storage Usage

To provide a personalized experience, VoiceFlow uses the chrome.storage.local API to store a small amount of data directly on your device. This data includes:

  • User Preferences: Your selected narrator voice and reading speed.
  • Trial Status: The date of installation to manage the 100-day free trial period.

This data is stored locally in your browser and is accessible only by the extension.

4. Permissions Justification

VoiceFlow requests the following permissions to function:

  • tts: Used to synthesize speech from text.
  • contextMenus: Used to add the "Read Selection Aloud" option to your right-click menu.
  • storage: Used to save your voice and speed settings locally.
  • activeTab/scripting: Used to create the visual highlight overlay on the page you are currently reading.

5. Third-Party Services

VoiceFlow utilizes the built-in Chrome Text-to-Speech engine. The audio is generated by the browser's native API. We do not share any user data with third-party analytics or advertising companies.

6. Your Rights

Since all data is stored locally on your machine, you have full control over it. You can clear all VoiceFlow data by simply uninstalling the extension from your browser.
